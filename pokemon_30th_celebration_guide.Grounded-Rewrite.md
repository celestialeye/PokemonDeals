## Executive Summary & Drop Strategy

The Pokémon Trading Card Game marks its 30th anniversary with the worldwide launch of *30th Celebration* on **Wednesday, September 16, 2026**. This set represents the first simultaneous global release in Pokémon TCG history. Packs feature an all-foil configuration (5 foil expansion cards, 1 foil Basic Energy, and 1 TCG Live code card), guaranteed Pikachu slots drawn from 30 unique commemorative illustrations, Classic Collection chase reprints, and the debut of the "Futuristic Rare" rarity.

Because booster packs are not sold in standalone 36-pack booster boxes for special expansions, all packs must be acquired via collection boxes, tins, and Elite Trainer Boxes (ETBs). 

### Core Drop Day Directives
1. **Physical Priority over Digital Assumptions:** Online allocations at Pokémon Center sold out during initial preorders on July 15, 2026. Any release-day online stock on September 16 will be unannounced and heavily contested by automated bots. Your highest probability of securing product at MSRP is holding an early physical line spot at high-allocation brick-and-mortar stores.
2. **Target Factoria Line Commitment:** Target Factoria (Store #339) is the highest-volume retail target in the immediate Eastside corridor. Arriving at **6:00 AM** secures a top-three spot at the entrance, ensuring first access when doors open at 8:00 AM.
3. **The 10:00 AM Retail Choice:** Both Best Buy Bellevue and GameStop Crossroads open at 10:00 AM on Wednesdays. Prioritize **Best Buy Bellevue** immediately following Target, as big-box consumer electronics stores receive significantly deeper master-case allocations than mall-based video game retailers. Follow Best Buy with Mox Boarding House (opens 11:00 AM) for premier local game store allocations.
4. **Purchase Limit Readiness:** Target and Best Buy enforce strict anti-scalping purchase limits. Expect store managers to enforce a hard cap of **one or two items total per guest** rather than per SKU.
